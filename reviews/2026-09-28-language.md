| Afrikaans | Spread 7 | "Sy het geswaai en gezoem... Ek onthou die *gezoem van die wind*" | "gesuis" / "geruis" instead of "gezoem" | "Gezoem" uses Dutch spelling (z, -oem); standard Afrikaans for a soft wind sound is closer to "gesuis" or "geruis". "Gezoem"/"zoem" in Afrikaans usually means a buzz (like an insect or phone), not wind. Flag for native check, not a fix. |
| Afrikaans | Spread 9 | "Die wind het gezoem" | same as above | Same word repeated; if Spread 7 changes, this should change too for consistency. |
| Afrikaans | Spread 8 | "Skilpaaie kom ook uiteindelik daar" | "Skilpaddens" / "Ons kom ook uiteindelik daar" | Plural of "skilpad" (tortoise) is usually "skilpaaie" is actually attested as an informal plural in some dialects, but "skilpaddens" is more standard; alternatively the line could just be rephrased to avoid the plural entirely. Lower-confidence flag than the "gezoem" one; worth a native check either way. |
| Afrikaans | Throughout | "Reënvoël" | no change suggested | Compound reads correctly as "rain bird". Flag only to confirm this isn't already a real species name that would clash with the invented character. |

A native speaker should confirm "ukuconsa" is the intended image here rather than a liquid-based word carried over loosely from the frogs' "drum" passage. |
