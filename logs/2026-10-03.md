## Picture-book manuscript draft: Episode 6 (new)

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Market of Small Kindnesses
**Format aim:** ~12 spreads, ages 4-8, soft bedtime. Amazon KDP or other POD. Illustrated characters only.
**Status:** First full manuscript draft for Episode 6. Aligns with YouTube handoff at `handoff/youtube/2026-10-03.md`. Refine later; do not restart from scratch once approved.
**Safety:** No child's face, real name, school, or location.
**Placeholders:** Tilly the tortoise, Kingdom of Sunhill, Mallow the Market Mouse (new). Bonayo may appear as a brief optional cameo only.

### English

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Market of Small Kindnesses

**Spread 1**
Market morning woke the Kingdom of Sunhill.
Banners fluttered. Baskets filled. Soft dust shone in the light.
"Today I will do one huge kindness!" Baylin called, racing ahead with empty hands and a very big plan.
Tilly the tortoise smiled and followed, humming.

**Spread 2**
Baylin piled fruit, cloth, and ribbons into her arms for a grand share.
"Everyone will see how kind I am," she said, almost running.
Tilly hummed, "The market already knows how to share, little by little."

**Spread 3**
At the first stall, the big plan spilled.
A soft fruit rolled. A basket tipped. Nothing broke. No one was hurt.
Baylin's cheeks warmed. "My huge kindness fell apart."

**Spread 4**
From a warm bun stall, Mallow the Market Mouse peeked out.
"Here," Mallow said, offering a tiny bun still soft from the oven. "Small is enough."
Baylin blinked. The bun fit in one hand and smelled like morning.

**Spread 5**
Tilly pointed with a gentle nod.
"Look what you rushed past," she hummed.
A shade cloth shared between two stalls. A turned basket set right. A smile for a shy shopper waiting their turn.

**Spread 6**
Baylin put down the huge plan.
She stood still and watched the market's soft rhythm.
Hands offering. Paws passing. Kindness moving like quiet water from stall to stall.

**Spread 7**
The mid cue floated over the awnings: "Share... soft and free."
Baylin offered one small kindness of her own: she returned a rolled fruit, then thanked Mallow with a careful smile.
No grand speech. Just one soft gift.

**Spread 8**
One small share invited another.
A neighbour passed a ripe pear down the line. An illustrated friend tied a ribbon on a frayed basket handle.
The market felt warmer, as if the morning itself had been shared.

**Spread 9**
Baylin, Tilly, and Mallow sat stall-side with tiny buns.
High above, Bonayo the owl glided once, watching kindly, then drifted on (a soft cameo, nothing more).
"I wanted one huge kindness," Baylin said. "I almost missed all the small ones."

**Spread 10**
"Your huge plan was kind," Mallow said softly. "But the market is made of small gifts that add up."
Tilly hummed in agreement. "Share... soft and free."
Baylin nodded. The stalls glowed like a string of little lanterns.

**Spread 11**
Baylin whispered her bedtime thank-yous.
"Thank you, friends. Thank you, Mallow. Thank you, Tilly."
The market hummed back, warm and quiet under softening light.

**Spread 12**
On the last page Baylin asked, "Who could you share one small kindness with today?"
Then she closed her eyes.
And the kingdom slept under a soft market-dusk sky, lanterns gentle, never scary.

**On-page text options:** "Share... soft and free" · "Thank you, friends!"
