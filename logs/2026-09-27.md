### English

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song

**Moral:** Listening well and working together: everyone carries a small piece of the answer.

**Premise:** It hasn't rained in the Kingdom of Sunhill for a long time, and the garden flowers are drooping. Everyone knows the Rainbird's song calls the rain, but the Rainbird has forgotten it. Baylin tries to fix things her usual way, by being quick and brave: she runs to the top of the hill and sings as loudly as she can. Nothing happens. This problem can't be solved by being fast or bold. Baylin has to slow down and listen, because every creature in the kingdom remembers just one small part of the song.

**Beat outline**
1. Dry morning: Baylin and Tilly find the garden flowers drooping and the pond very low.
2. The Rainbird sits sadly on the old fig tree. "I've forgotten my rain song."
3. Baylin rushes to the hilltop and sings loudly. Only a puff of dust answers. She feels frustrated.
4. Tilly, slow and calm, says: "Maybe we need to listen, not shout."
5. Baylin visits the frogs (who remember the *drum* of the rain), the grasshoppers (the *patter*) and the grandmother tree (the *hum of the wind*). She asks gentle questions and listens carefully.
6. Baylin gets impatient and nearly gives up, until Tilly reminds her that tortoises get there in the end.
7. The pieces come together: the frogs drum, the grasshoppers patter, the wind hums, and the Rainbird remembers the tune.
8. Everyone sings together. Clouds gather and soft rain falls, gently and not scarily.
9. Splashing in puddles. Baylin says thank you to each friend. "The song was never lost. It was shared out."
10. Closing line and invitation: "What small thing can you do to help your friends today?"

**On-screen text:** "Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song" · "Listen… what do you hear?" · "Thank you, friends!"
