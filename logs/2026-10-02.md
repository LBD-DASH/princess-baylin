**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Very Patient Tortoise
**Format aim:** ~12 spreads, ages 4-8, soft bedtime. Amazon KDP or other POD. Illustrated characters only.
**Status:** First full manuscript draft for Episode 5. Aligns with YouTube handoff at `handoff/youtube/2026-10-02.md`. Refine later; do not restart from scratch once approved.

### English

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Very Patient Tortoise

**Spread 1**
Late afternoon light warmed the hillside above the Kingdom of Sunhill.
"Come on, Tilly!" Baylin called. "The warm light won't wait for the lookout!"
Tilly the tortoise smiled. "It waits for those who walk with it," she hummed, but Baylin was already climbing.

**Spread 2**
Baylin raced up the path, dust soft under her feet.
Tilly followed at her own pace, humming a slow, steady tune.
The warm light stretched long and golden across the grass.

**Spread 3**
The path forked. One way was steep and fast through tall grass. The other wound gently past painted pebbles.
"This way is quicker!" Baylin said, and took the steep shortcut alone.
Tilly stayed on the winding path, still humming.

**Spread 4**
Tall grass closed softly around Baylin. Soft, not scary. But every way looked the same.
She turned left. Then right. The lookout felt farther away than before.
"Tilly?" she called. The grass only whispered back.

**Spread 5**
On the slow path, Tilly noticed little painted pebbles pointing the way.
A thin snail trail shone beside a wind marker that fluttered like a friendly finger.
"Ah," Tilly said softly. "The path is talking. I will listen."

**Spread 6**
From the tall grass, Baylin heard it: Tilly's hum, soft and steady.
She stopped thrashing. She stood still and listened for her friend.
The hum was not ahead of her. It was beside her, on the gentle path.

**Spread 7**
The mid cue floated over the hillside: "Walk... soft and slow."
Baylin stepped carefully toward the sound, one soft step at a time.
No racing. No shortcut. Just listening feet.

**Spread 8**
Baylin found Tilly on the winding path among the painted pebbles.
"I went too fast," Baylin said. "I left you behind."
"You left the path too," Tilly hummed. "Shall we walk it together?"

**Spread 9**
Baylin matched her steps to Tilly's. Slow. Soft. Side by side.
The wind markers nodded them around the last bend.
And then the hilltop lookout opened like a wide, warm window.

**Spread 10**
Honey-gold light poured over the kingdom below.
"I wasn't late," Tilly said softly. "I was with the path. And now I'm with you."
Baylin nodded. The view felt bigger because they shared it.

**Spread 11**
Baylin whispered her bedtime thank-yous.
"Thank you, friends. Thank you, Tilly."
The hillside hummed back, warm and quiet.

**Spread 12**
On the last page Baylin asked, "Who could you walk beside today?"
Then she closed her eyes.
And the kingdom slept under a soft honey evening sky.

**On-page text options:** "Walk... soft and slow" · "Thank you, friends!"
