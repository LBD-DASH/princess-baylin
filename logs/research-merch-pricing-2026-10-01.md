# Merch pricing research, 2026-10-01

Comparable Etsy listings for the five merch concepts in logs/2026-09-30.md. Previously blocked (no web access, 28-30 Sep); web tools worked this run. Prices as returned by live web search, in USD unless noted; convert to ZAR at Kevin's own rate when pricing the listing.

## 1. Sleepy Moon colouring page (concept #1)

Comparable: moon/night-sky kids colouring page digital downloads.
- [Phases of the Moon Coloring Page](https://www.etsy.com/listing/882512592/phases-of-the-moon-coloring-page)
- [Sun and Moon Coloring Pages Bundle](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1050591939/sun-and-moon-coloring-pages-instant)
- Price band seen: $1.25-$5.76 for single/small-bundle moon colouring pages.
- Draft note's R40-R75 (roughly $2.20-$4.10) sits inside this band.

## 2. Quiet Star colouring page (concept #2)

Comparable: star-themed kids colouring page digital downloads.
- [Rainbows, Hearts, Stars Printable Coloring Page](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1762047957/rainbows-hearts-stars-printable-coloring)
- [Simple Star Coloring Page](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4462243373/simple-star-coloring-page-easy-outline)
- Price band seen: $0.55-$2.50 for a single star-themed page.
- Draft note's R40-R75 (~$2.20-$4.10) is at or above the top of this band; a single-page star colouring sheet alone may be priced generously. Consider bundling with concept #1 to justify the price, or pricing nearer R40.

## 3. Printable star-card set (concept #3)

Comparable: bedtime affirmation/ritual card sets for kids.
- [Bedtime Affirmations for Kids](https://www.etsy.com/listing/990616668/bedtime-affirmations-for-kids-positive)
- Price band seen: $0.78-$3.39 for a small affirmation/ritual card set.
- Draft note's R45-R85 (~$2.50-$4.70) is above this band. A trilingual (EN/AF/ZU) angle is a real differentiator not seen in the comps, which may support pricing at the top of or slightly above the comp band once native-speaker-checked.

## 4. Soft night-sky art print (concept #4)

Comparable: night-sky/celestial nursery wall art digital downloads (not decals/murals).
- Night Sky Fireflies Print: $8.50 (digital download) — found via search, no direct listing URL captured.
- [Personalized Baby Star Map Print](https://www.etsy.com/market/night_sky_baby) (market page; star map prints around $7.22)
- Price band seen: $3-$10 for single-sheet digital art prints; $25-$40 only for physical decals/murals (not this concept).
- Draft note's R180-R350 (~$10-$19) is above the digital-download comp band. Either this is priced as a small physical print run (framed or posted), which the comps don't cover, or the draft price should come down for a PDF-only art print.

## 5. Trilingual goodnight poster (concept #5)

Comparable: goodnight/nursery posters; true trilingual (EN/AF/ZU) comps not found.
- [Goodnight Print, Bonne Nuit Poster](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4326313620/goodnight-print-bonne-nuit-poster)
- [Goodnight Posters, Nursery Digital Prints](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1819820781/goodnight-posters-nursery-digital-prints)
- Price band seen: roughly $2.79-$28.05 for goodnight-themed nursery prints (digital downloads cluster $5-$15); the one bilingual (French) example found was $3.99-$16.85.
- Draft note's R60-R120 (~$3.30-$6.60) sits in the lower-middle of this band. No competing isiZulu or Afrikaans goodnight poster was found in this search, which is a genuine gap/opportunity, not just a guess.

## Overall takeaway

Concepts #1 and #5 are priced in line with comparable Etsy listings. Concepts #2 and #3 may be priced slightly high for a single-item digital download unless bundled. Concept #4's price band assumes something beyond a plain PDF download (comps for PDF-only prints are $3-$10, well under the R180-R350 / ~$10-$19 draft). None of this is a listing decision; still draft-only, no listings created.
