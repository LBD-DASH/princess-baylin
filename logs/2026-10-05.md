## Picture-book manuscript: Episode 2 light refine (Day 9)

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Sleepy Moon
**Format aim:** ~12 spreads, ages 4-8, soft bedtime. Amazon KDP or other POD. Illustrated characters only.
**Status:** Light refine of the Day 4 Ep2 draft in `logs/2026-09-30.md` (first draft `logs/2026-09-29.md`). Aligns with today's production pack in `handoff/youtube/2026-10-05.md`. Plot, lesson and setting unchanged.
**Safety:** No child's face, real name, school, or location.
**Placeholders:** Tilly the tortoise, Kingdom of Sunhill, Sleepy Moon, Quiet Star. Bonayo is canon.

### What changed from the Day 4 draft
- Spread 2: the moon now says what it wants (one slow story), matching approved beat 5.
- Spread 3: Baylin's rushing now includes racing through a too-fast story, the gentle comedy beat from approved beat 6.
- Spread 6: the slow story is now about soft rain and friends (light Ep1 callback from approved beat 8) instead of a hill.
- Spreads 6 and 9: the "one quiet star" is now the named Quiet Star, blinking once per pause.
- Spread 12: Bonayo's soft "Hoo-hoo" goodnight added to match the locked bedtime close; AI disclosure line added.
- Unchanged: cast, lesson (patience), night setting, mid cue "Wait... soft and slow", thank-you ritual, closing question.

### English

**Spread 1**
Night settled over the Kingdom of Sunhill like a soft blanket.
Princess Baylin was ready for bed. Her pillow was cool. Her window was open.
But high above the hills, the Sleepy Moon would not sleep.

**Spread 2**
The moon blinked and blinked. The sky felt wide and awake.
"I want to rest," whispered the Sleepy Moon, "but first I need one slow story."
Baylin sat up. "I can help! I'm good at fixing things fast."

**Spread 3**
Baylin rushed. She clapped for quiet. She shushed the breeze.
Then she told a story so fast the words tumbled over each other:
"Once-there-was-a-bunny-the-end!"
The moon only blinked brighter.

**Spread 4**
Baylin huffed. "Why won't you sleep?"
Tilly the tortoise blinked slowly beside the bed.
"When I hurry," Tilly said, "my shell feels heavy. When I wait... soft and slow... rest comes."

**Spread 5**
Baylin tried again, softer this time.
She sat by the window. She did not clap. She did not rush.
Together they whispered: "Wait... soft and slow."
The words floated up like a tiny cloud.

**Spread 6**
A small star by the moon gave one slow blink. It was the Quiet Star.
Baylin began a slow story about soft rain and good friends.
After every line, she waited. And after every wait, the Quiet Star blinked.

**Spread 7**
The Sleepy Moon's blinks grew longer.
"That story feels like a pillow," said the moon.
Baylin smiled. "We can wait with you. We do not need to hurry the night."

**Spread 8**
Outside, the hills held their breath.
Inside, Baylin's room grew cosy and dim.
Tilly rested her shell against the bedpost. Soft. Slow.

**Spread 9**
Baylin finished the story with a whisper.
The Quiet Star blinked one last, long blink.
The Sleepy Moon yawned a silver yawn, and its eye drifted closed.

**Spread 10**
The night grew calm. Stars shone like night-lights, not like fireworks.
"Thank you for waiting with me," murmured the Sleepy Moon.
Baylin nodded. "Waiting was the kind thing."

**Spread 11**
Baylin whispered her bedtime thank-yous.
"Thank you, Tilly. Thank you, Quiet Star. Thank you, Sleepy Moon."
"Thank you, friends!"

**Spread 12**
From the old fig came a soft "Hoo-hoo." Bonayo the owl was keeping watch.
On the last page Baylin asked, "What is one thing you can wait for gently today?"
Then she closed her eyes,
and the kingdom slept under a soft, sleepy moon.

**On-page text options:** "Wait... soft and slow" / "Thank you, friends!"
