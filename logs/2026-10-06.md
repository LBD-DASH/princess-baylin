### English

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Quiet Star

**Spread 1**
Dusk painted the Kingdom of Sunhill in peach and soft blue.
Princess Baylin and Tilly the tortoise walked the garden path.
Big bright stars already shone. One tiny light hid behind a cloud.

**Spread 2**
Baylin almost walked past. Tilly stopped.
"Look again," Tilly said softly. "Soft and small."
Baylin looked, carefully this time. A shy glow peeked out. "Hello, little light. We nearly missed you."

**Spread 3**
"I am the Quiet Star," it whispered. "I am too small to help anyone.
The bright stars guide ships. I only blink."
Baylin smiled. "You are a light. Even a small one. Maybe small is exactly what we need."

**Spread 4**
Baylin wanted to help, big and bright.
She danced. She waved two garden lanterns, one in each hand.
The Quiet Star slipped deeper behind the cloud. "Oh," said Baylin. "I think I was being loud with light."

**Spread 5**
Tilly stepped closer, one careful foot at a time.
"Big lights are lovely," she said. "But sometimes a friend needs a soft glow close by.
Bravery can be quiet." Baylin set the lanterns down.

**Spread 6**
In the tall grass, a little firefly zigzagged, paused, and zigzagged again.
It had lost the path home, and the bright stars were far too high to help.
Baylin sat very still. "Quiet Star, will you try one soft blink?"

**Spread 7**
For a moment, nothing moved.
Then a pale gold tip of light peeked out. "Shine... soft and small," Baylin whispered.
One gentle blink. Then another, a little braver each time.

**Spread 8**
Tiny blinks marked the grass like stepping stones.
The firefly followed them, wing by careful wing, all the way home.
No chase. No fear. Only quiet kindness.

**Spread 9**
"My light was enough," said the Quiet Star, surprised.
"Small can still guide," said Baylin. "Quiet courage counts."
Tilly nodded. "The brightest star is not always the one a friend needs."

**Spread 10**
High above, the Sleepy Moon winked, just once.
The garden felt safe and kind.
Baylin and Tilly walked home slowly, with no hurry at all.

**Spread 11**
In bed, Baylin whispered her bedtime thank-yous.
"Thank you, friends. Thank you, Tilly. Thank you, Quiet Star."
Outside her window, one small star still glowed.

**Spread 12**
On the last page Baylin asked, "What small kindness can you share today?"
Then she closed her eyes.
And the Quiet Star kept a soft watch while the kingdom dreamed.

**On-page text options:** "Shine... soft and small" / "Thank you, friends!"
