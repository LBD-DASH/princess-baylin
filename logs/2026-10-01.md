**Title:** Princess Baylin and the River That Whispered
**Format aim:** ~12 spreads, ages 4-8, soft bedtime. Amazon KDP or other POD. Illustrated characters only.
**Status:** First full manuscript draft for Episode 4. Aligns with YouTube handoff at `handoff/youtube/2026-10-01.md`. Refine later; do not restart from scratch once approved.

### English

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the River That Whispered

**Spread 1**
Morning light danced on the river at the edge of the Kingdom of Sunhill.
"Race you to the willow tree!" Baylin called, already running.
Tilly the tortoise sighed. "We'll get there in the end," she said, but Baylin was already gone.

**Spread 2**
Baylin's feet splashed through the shallow reeds, quick and loud.
She did not see the little home of woven reeds and smooth pebbles tucked by the bank.
Pebbles scattered. Reeds bent and broke.

**Spread 3**
"Oh!" A small voice bubbled up from the water. "My home... it's all come apart."
A tiny river fish named Pip poked his head above the ripples, looking at the mess.
Baylin skidded to a stop. "Sorry!" she called over her shoulder, and kept running for the willow tree.

**Spread 4**
Tilly caught up slowly and looked at the scattered pebbles.
"Baylin," she said gently, "I think your sorry ran away before you did."
Baylin stopped. She looked back. Pip was still staring sadly at his broken home.

**Spread 5**
Baylin's cheeks went warm. She had said sorry, but she had not stopped.
"A sorry that keeps running isn't finished yet," said Tilly.
Slowly, Baylin walked back along the bank.

**Spread 6**
"I'm sorry, Pip," Baylin said again, kneeling down this time. "Will you show me what broke?"
Pip blinked. No one had asked that before.
"The current used to whisper through my reeds," Pip said quietly. "Now it just... bumps."

**Spread 7**
The mid cue floated over the water: "Mend... soft and true."
Baylin picked up one smooth pebble. Then another. She placed them slowly, the way Pip showed her.
No rushing. No racing. Just soft, careful hands.

**Spread 8**
Tilly hummed while she passed Baylin the reeds, one at a time.
Baylin wove them the way Pip remembered, bending them soft instead of forcing them fast.
The little home began to take its old shape again.

**Spread 9**
When the last pebble settled into place, the current slipped through the reeds.
It made the softest sound, like a tiny whispering song.
"There it is," Pip said, smiling. "The river is whispering again."

**Spread 10**
"I'm glad we fixed it together," Baylin said.
"A true sorry has hands that help," said Pip, "not just words that hurry past."
Baylin nodded. She would remember that.

**Spread 11**
Baylin whispered her bedtime thank-yous.
"Thank you, friends. Thank you, Tilly. Thank you, Pip."
The river whispered softly back, like it was saying goodnight too.

**Spread 12**
On the last page Baylin asked, "What could you help mend today?"
Then she closed her eyes.
And the kingdom slept beside a river that whispered, soft and true.

**On-page text options:** "Mend… soft and true" · "Thank you, friends!"
