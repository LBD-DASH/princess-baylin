## Picture-book manuscript: Episode 1 refine (Day 11)

**Book:** Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song. 8.5 x 8.5 in, 26 pages (title, credits, then 12 spreads). Text lives in `book/ep1-lost-rain-song/text.md`, `text-af.md` and `text-zu.md`; the KDP PDFs in `out/` were built from those files. **Today's edits are proposed here and not yet applied to the book files**, so the PDFs and the text stay in step. Apply them at the next rebuild (when Kevin answers the KDP questions).

### Why these edits
Now that the Ep1 video is going public, the book should not contradict it:
1. **Page 26 "Next time" line.** The video closes with "Princess Baylin meets the Sleepy Moon, and learns that waiting can be as brave as running." That matches the approved Ep2 video plot (the moon is late to rise). The book only says "Next time: Princess Baylin and the Sleepy Moon." Adding the waiting line ties book and video together and does not commit to either Ep2 book plot beyond what the approved video already says.
2. **Page 25 Bonayo.** The book has Bonayo's "Hoo-hoo" goodnight, but the page art (frame `s12_585.75`) has no owl, because the video has none. One added line makes him heard, not seen, so the text and the picture agree. He stays named (Kevin's canon) and no new art is needed.
Everything else in the Day 8 text already matches the approved video (hook, the three song pieces, "Maybe I am not enough", "Tortoises get there in the end", "The song was never lost. It was shared out.", "Thank you, friends!").

- Reviewer question: does "net so dapper ... soos hardloop" sound natural to a young child, or is "waar wag ook 'n soort dapperheid is" warmer?

### isiZulu - NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK (changed lines only)

- Page 25, added after "...sivuka ebusuku.": "Sasicashe ekujuleni kwamaqabunga, kodwa sasizwile yonke ingoma."
  (Uses the isikhova noun-class agreement si-, matching the existing line "isikhova esihlakaniphile esidala, sivuka". Literally "it was hidden deep in the leaves, but it had heard the whole song." "Every note" has no easy word, so "the whole song" is used.)
- Page 26, replaces the "Ngokuzayo" line: "Ngokuzayo: Inkosazana uBaylin neNyanga Eleleyo, lapho ukulinda kunesibindi njengokugijima."
- Reviewer questions: is "ukulinda kunesibindi njengokugijima" understandable for ages 3 to 7, or should it be "ukulinda nakho kuyisibindi"? The Ep2 isiZulu title still has three unchecked options (neNyanga Eleleyo / elele / Ezozelayo); keep the one the reviewer picks here too.
