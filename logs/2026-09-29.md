### English

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song

**Spread 1**
In the Kingdom of Sunhill, mornings used to sparkle with dew.
Today the flowers hung their heads, and the pond was almost empty.
Princess Baylin rested a hand on Tilly the tortoise's shell and whispered, "Something is wrong."

**Spread 2**
High in the old fig tree sat the Rainbird.
Her feathers drooped like the tired flowers.
"I've forgotten my rain song," she said. "Without it, the clouds won't come."

**Spread 3**
Baylin knew what to do. She was quick. She was brave.
She ran to the top of the hill and sang as loudly as she could.
A tiny puff of dust answered. Nothing else.

**Spread 4**
Baylin stamped her foot. "Why won't it work?"
Tilly blinked slowly. "Maybe we need to listen," she said, "not shout."

**Spread 5**
By the pond, the frogs sat on lily pads.
Baylin asked softly, "Do you remember any of the rain song?"
The frogs smiled. "We remember the *drum*."
*Dum-dum. Dum-dum.* Soft as a heartbeat.

**Spread 6**
In the long grass, the grasshoppers waited.
"We remember the *patter*," they chirped.
*Tip-tip-tip.* Like tiny feet on dry leaves.

**Spread 7**
At the edge of the garden stood the grandmother tree.
She swayed and hummed. "I remember the *hum of the wind*."
It sounded like a lullaby for clouds.

**Spread 8**
Baylin sat on a warm rock. Her pockets of song felt small.
"What if it still isn't enough?"
Tilly rested beside her. "Tortoises get there in the end," she said. "So can we."

**Spread 9**
They brought every piece to the Rainbird.
Frogs drummed. Grasshoppers pattered. The wind hummed.
The Rainbird tilted her head… and the tune came home.

**Spread 10**
Together they sang. Soft clouds gathered, kind and grey.
Rain fell gently, like a thousand quiet thank-yous.
The flowers lifted their faces. The pond began to fill.

**Spread 11**
Baylin and Tilly splashed in the cool puddles.
"Thank you," Baylin told each friend.
"The song was never lost. It was shared out."

**Spread 12**
That night Baylin whispered goodnight to the sky.
On the last page she asked, "What small thing can you do to help your friends today?"
And the kingdom slept, cool and green again.

**On-page text options:** "Listen… what do you hear?" · "Thank you, friends!"

### English

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Sleepy Moon

**Spread 1**
Night settled over the Kingdom of Sunhill like a soft blanket.
Princess Baylin was ready for bed. Her pillow was cool. Her window was open.
But high above the hills, the Sleepy Moon would not sleep.

**Spread 2**
The moon blinked and blinked. Stars winked. The sky felt wide and awake.
"I want to rest," whispered the Sleepy Moon, "but my eyes keep wanting to look."
Baylin sat up. "I can help! I'm good at fixing things fast."

**Spread 3**
Baylin rushed. She clapped for quiet. She shushed the breeze.
She waved a blanket at the sky as if she could tuck the moon in at once.
The moon only blinked faster. The night stayed bright and busy.

**Spread 4**
Baylin huffed. "Why won't you sleep?"
Tilly the tortoise blinked slowly beside the bed.
"When I hurry," Tilly said, "my shell feels heavy. When I wait… soft and slow… rest comes."

**Spread 5**
Baylin tried again, softer this time.
She sat by the window. She did not clap. She did not rush.
"Wait… soft and slow," she whispered with Tilly.
The mid cue floated up like a tiny cloud.

**Spread 6**
They watched one quiet star. Then another.
Baylin told a slow story about a hill that learned to stay still.
Tilly hummed a tiny tune, as slow as a tortoise step.

**Spread 7**
The Sleepy Moon's blinks grew longer.
"That story feels like a pillow," said the moon.
Baylin smiled. "We can wait with you. We do not need to hurry the night."

**Spread 8**
Outside, the hills held their breath.
Inside, Baylin's room grew cosy and dim.
Tilly rested her shell against the bedpost. Soft. Slow.

**Spread 9**
Baylin finished the story with a whisper.
The Sleepy Moon yawned a silver yawn.
One by one, the busy blinks became gentle dreams.

**Spread 10**
The night grew calm. Stars shone like night-lights, not like fireworks.
"Thank you for waiting with me," said the Sleepy Moon.
Baylin nodded. "Thank you for teaching us soft and slow."

**Spread 11**
Baylin whispered her bedtime thank-yous.
"Thank you, friends. Thank you, Tilly. Thank you, Sleepy Moon."
The room felt full of quiet kindness.

**Spread 12**
On the last page Baylin asked, "What can you wait for gently today?"
Then she closed her eyes.
And the kingdom slept under a soft, sleepy moon.

**On-page text options:** "Wait… soft and slow" · "Thank you, friends!"
