#### English (~12 spreads, lightly polished Day 13 switch draft)

**Title:** Princess Baylin and the Sleepy Moon

**Spread 1**
Night settled over the Kingdom of Sunhill. Princess Baylin lay tucked under her blanket. Everything was ready for sleep.
She looked at her window. The curtains were open on a flat, starless navy sky. No moon. No little silver puddle on the floor.
"Where is the moon?" she whispered. "It always comes to say goodnight."

**Spread 2**
A gentle tap came at the door. Tilly the tortoise poked her head in, one careful step at a time.
"Still awake?" asked Tilly softly.
Baylin nodded. "Look at the floor. Usually the moon makes a silver puddle right there. Tonight there is nothing."
Tilly blinked slowly. "Hmm. The night is taking its time."

**Spread 3**
Baylin slipped out of bed. She pulled a soft cloak over her nightgown and tied it under her chin.
"The moon must be lost," she said. "Or stuck. Or forgotten the way to Sunhill. I will go and find it myself."
Tilly's eyes widened, slow as sunrise. "At night? Over the hill?"
"Yes," said Baylin, trying to sound brave. "Worrying in bed will not bring the moon any faster."

**Spread 4**
They climbed the hill outside the castle. The path was moonless and shadowy. Baylin walked too quickly. Her cloak flapped.
Then she tripped over a root and sat down with a little bump. She was not hurt. Only surprised.
"The hill is being unhelpful," she said.
Tilly arrived behind her, one steady step after another. "The hill is the same as always. We are the ones in a hurry."

**Spread 5**
From a bare branch above them came a calm voice. "Hoo. Looking for the moon, little princess?"
Baylin looked up. There sat Bonayo, a round owl with soft amber eyes. Bonayo looked like a soft night lamp with feathers.
"Yes," said Baylin. "It is late. Can you tell us where it went?"
Bonayo tilted its head kindly. "The moon rises when it is ready, not when it is called. Worry cannot pull it up. Only time can."

**Spread 6**
Baylin cupped her hands around her mouth and called as loudly as she could into the dark sky.
"Moon! Moon! Please hurry! We are waiting in Sunhill!"
She called again, louder.
And then... nothing. No moon. No silver. Only the quiet dark.
Baylin's cheeks felt warm. She was frustrated, but she was still safe, and Tilly was still beside her.

**Spread 7**
Tilly found a flat stone and settled onto it with a soft little shell sound.
"Perhaps," said Tilly, "we stop trying to fetch it. Perhaps we sit and wait together."
She patted the space beside her with one careful foot.
Baylin hesitated. Sitting felt like doing nothing. Then she looked at Tilly's calm face, and she sat down.
"We will wait," Baylin said. "But I am not good at waiting."
"That is all right," said Tilly. "Waiting is a skill you practise."

**Spread 8**
They sat side by side and looked up.
"Look," whispered Tilly. "The first star."
A tiny light blinked on in the navy sky.
"One," said Baylin.
Another blinked on. "Two." Then a third. "Three."
Count with us… how many stars?
One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Soft little lights, arriving when they are ready.
Baylin's shoulders lowered. Counting made the waiting feel like a gentle game.

**Spread 9**
After a while Baylin hugged her knees.
"What if the moon forgot about Sunhill altogether?" she asked.
Tilly stayed steady beside her.
"The moon has not forgotten," said Tilly. "It is only sleepy. Sleepy things take longer to wake. That does not mean they are gone."
"I will try to trust it," Baylin whispered.

**Spread 10**
Then something changed. At the edge of the world, where the hill met the sky, a faint silver glow began.
"Tilly," breathed Baylin. "Do you see that?"
"I see it," said Tilly.
The glow grew, slowly, kindly. Navy softened toward silver. Baylin did not jump up. She did not shout. She watched.

**Spread 11**
And then the full moon rose over the Kingdom of Sunhill.
It was round and calm and bright enough to make the hillside soft. Bonayo's amber eyes glowed like tiny friendly lanterns.
"It came," she said. "It really came."
"Thank you for waiting with me," she told Tilly.
"Thank you for sitting," said Tilly. "Sitting is harder than running, sometimes."

**Spread 12**
They walked home calmly in the moonlight. Back in her room, moonlight made a soft puddle on the floor, exactly where it belonged.
"Thank you, friends!" said Baylin.
"What can you wait for patiently today?" she asked softly.
Perhaps a turn. Waiting is a quiet kind of bravery.
And if you listen carefully tomorrow night, a small light may learn that it can be enough.
Goodnight, little listener. Sleep well. The moon will keep watch softly while you dream.
