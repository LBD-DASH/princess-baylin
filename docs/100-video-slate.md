# Princess Baylin Diaries: 100-video slate

Approved by Kevin on 2026-11-14 SAST. One hundred videos over 20 weeks from Monday 23 November 2026: 40 story episodes (Tuesday and Friday), 40 Calm Minute Shorts (Wednesday and Saturday, the day after each episode), 10 Sunhill Songs (Sunday, odd weeks) and 10 Sleepy-Time Collections (Sunday, even weeks).

## How to use this file

- `docs/100-video-slate.csv` holds the same rows with every column. Agents read the CSV. When a script is written or a video ships, change the `status` and `script_file` cells in the CSV and the matching line here, in the same commit.
- Kevin's working copy is a private Claude Sheet. If the two disagree, the Sheet wins on dates and status, because Kevin edits it by hand.
- Status values, in order: Idea, Script drafted, Script approved, In production, Ready to publish, Live.
- Every cast and place name except Baylin and Bonayo is a placeholder until Kevin and the author check the slate against the original story. Afrikaans and isiZulu names need the native-speaker check like everything else.
- No two episodes share a lesson, a calm cue or a new friend. The 40 stories use 18 story shapes and none is used more than 4 times, so the channel never puts Baylin through the same situation and outcome on a repeated template. Keep that true when a row changes.
- If the launch slips, move every date by whole weeks. The weekday pattern stays.

## The 40 stories

### Season 1: The Garden and the Night Sky

**S1E01. Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song** (Tue 24 Nov 2026, Live)

- Lesson: Listening
- New friend: The Rainbird
- Setting: The dry palace garden
- What happens: The garden is thirsty because the Rainbird has forgotten her rain song. Shouting does not bring it back. Baylin collects the pieces from the frogs, the grasshoppers and the Grandmother Tree, and everyone sings it home together.
- Calm cue: "Listen... what do you hear?" Story shape: Quest.
- Calm Minute Short (SH01, Wed 25 Nov 2026): the child is asked, "Close your eyes and name one sound you can hear."
- Script: faceless-youtube-content `scripts/2026-10-04.md`

**S1E02. Princess Baylin and the Sleepy Moon** (Fri 27 Nov 2026, Script approved)

- Lesson: Patience
- New friend: The Sleepy Moon
- Setting: The hill under the night sky
- What happens: The moon is late to rise and Baylin sets out to fetch it. Bonayo and Tilly teach her to sit and wait. She counts the first stars, and the moon comes up in its own time.
- Calm cue: "Count with us... how many stars?" Story shape: Waiting.
- Calm Minute Short (SH02, Sat 28 Nov 2026): the child is asked, "Count five stars on your fingers."
- Script: faceless-youtube-content `scripts/2026-10-05.md`

**S1E03. Princess Baylin and the Quiet Star** (Tue 1 Dec 2026, Script approved)

- Lesson: Quiet courage
- New friend: The Quiet Star
- Setting: The garden path at dusk
- What happens: A small shy star hides behind the clouds, sure its light is too little to matter. When a firefly loses its way home, the Quiet Star's soft glow is the one light gentle enough to follow.
- Calm cue: "Shine... soft and small." Story shape: Guiding someone home.
- Calm Minute Short (SH03, Wed 2 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Make a small star with your hand and hold it up."
- Script: faceless-youtube-content `scripts/2026-10-06.md`

**S1E04. Princess Baylin and the River That Whispered** (Fri 4 Dec 2026, Script approved)

- Lesson: Making amends
- New friend: Pip the River Fish
- Setting: The sunlit riverbank
- What happens: Baylin's hurrying feet scatter Pip's tiny home of reeds and pebbles. A quick sorry changes nothing. She learns that a true sorry stays and helps, and rebuilds the home pebble by pebble.
- Calm cue: "Mend... soft and true." Story shape: Mistake and repair.
- Calm Minute Short (SH04, Sat 5 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Think of one thing you could help put right tomorrow."
- Script: faceless-youtube-content `scripts/2026-10-01.md`

**S1E05. Princess Baylin and the Very Patient Tortoise** (Tue 8 Dec 2026, Script approved)

- Lesson: Going at a friend's pace
- New friend: Tilly the Tortoise
- Setting: The hill path to the picnic meadow
- What happens: Baylin hurries ahead with the picnic basket so that everything will be ready for Tilly. A picnic is no fun alone. She walks back, matches Tilly's steps, and finds the gifts the fast path misses.
- Calm cue: "Slow... soft and kind." Story shape: Journey.
- Calm Minute Short (SH05, Wed 9 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Walk two fingers slowly up your arm, as slow as Tilly."
- Script: faceless-youtube-content `scripts/2026-10-02.md`

**S1E06. Princess Baylin and the Market of Small Kindnesses** (Fri 11 Dec 2026, Script approved)

- Lesson: Small kindnesses add up
- New friend: Mallow the Market Mouse
- Setting: Market morning in Sunhill
- What happens: Baylin plans one huge kindness and it spills at the first stall. Mallow shows her a tiny bun passed from paw to paw. One small share invites the next until the whole market is warmer.
- Calm cue: "Share... soft and free." Story shape: Many small acts.
- Calm Minute Short (SH06, Sat 12 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Share one smile with the person next to you."
- Script: faceless-youtube-content `scripts/2026-10-03.md`

**S1E07. Princess Baylin and the Thank-You Lantern** (Tue 15 Dec 2026, Script approved)

- Lesson: Saying thank you by name
- New friend: Wick the Hedgehog, the lantern keeper
- Setting: Lantern Night on the hill path
- What happens: On Lantern Night a lantern lights only for a thank-you with a name in it. Baylin's one big thank-you to everyone lights nothing. She thanks each friend for one true thing, and the last lantern turns out to be lit for her.
- Calm cue: "Thank... soft and warm." Story shape: Ritual with a rule.
- Calm Minute Short (SH07, Wed 16 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Say one name and one true thank-you."

- New friend: The Grandmother Tree
- Setting: The old fig tree at the year's end
- What happens: Everyone is wrapping gifts, but what do you give a tree? Ribbons and shiny stones do not fit. Baylin gives the Grandmother Tree a whole afternoon: sweeping her roots, telling her the year's stories, and simply staying.
- Calm cue: "Stay... soft and close." Story shape: Finding the right gift.
- Calm Minute Short (SH08, Sat 19 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Give someone a slow hug and count to five."

**S1E09. Princess Baylin and the Knot That Grew** (Tue 22 Dec 2026, Idea)

- Lesson: Asking for help
- New friend: Siza the Bushbaby
- Setting: The palace courtyard at dusk
- What happens: The festival ribbons tangle into one great knot, and the harder Baylin pulls, the tighter it gets. She hides it, then finds the courage to ask. Siza's small clever fingers show her that knots come loose slowly, and with help.
- Calm cue: "Ask... soft and brave." Story shape: Tangle to undo.
- Calm Minute Short (SH09, Wed 23 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Whisper, 'Please will you help me?'"

**S1E10. Princess Baylin and the Day That Did Not Want to End** (Fri 25 Dec 2026, Idea)

- Lesson: Rest, and letting the day end
- New friend: Every friend from Season 1
- Setting: The palace garden at nightfall
- What happens: It has been the best day and Baylin will not let it end. She keeps every friend awake with one more game until the whole garden is yawning. Bonayo shows her that goodnight is how the day folds up so that tomorrow can open.
- Calm cue: "Rest... soft and deep." Story shape: Letting go.
- Calm Minute Short (SH10, Sat 26 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Stretch, yawn and let your shoulders drop."

### Season 2: The Whispering Veld

- Setting: The open veld beyond the garden wall

- Calm cue: "Look... soft and far." Story shape: A friend teaches a skill.
- Calm Minute Short (SH11, Wed 30 Dec 2026): the child is asked, "Look around the room and find three things you had not noticed."

- New friend: Themba the Weaver Bird

- Calm cue: "Try... soft and new." Story shape: A friend's own struggle.
