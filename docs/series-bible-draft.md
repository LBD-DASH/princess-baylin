## Premise

Princess Baylin is the hero of a gentle children's fairy-tale world. Each story is a calm bedtime adventure in which Baylin and a friend meet one small problem and find a kind way through it together. Age range 4-8. Tone: warm, hand-drawn storybook feel, never scary, no distress-bait.

The stories do not share one plot. The slate uses 18 story shapes (a quest, a wait, a mistake and its repair, a ritual with a rule, finding the right gift, a friend's own struggle, a role reversal and others) and none is used more than four times. Baylin acting too fast is one of her flaws, not the engine of every episode: she does not open by racing ahead and close by slowing down again.

## World (placeholder, pending the original story)

- **Kingdom of Sunhill**: Baylin's home kingdom. Settings used so far: a dry garden (Ep 1), a hill under the night sky (Ep 2), a garden path at dusk (Ep 3), a sunlit riverbank (Ep 4), the hill path to the picnic meadow (Ep 5), market morning (Ep 6), Lantern Night on the hill path (Ep 7), the old fig tree at the year's end (Ep 8).
- **Four seasons, four parts of the kingdom:** the garden and the night sky (Episodes 1 to 10), the whispering veld beyond the garden wall (11 to 20), the river road to the sea (21 to 30) and the mountain of mist (31 to 40).
- **Customs introduced so far (placeholders):** Lantern Night, when a paper lantern lights only for a thank-you with a name in it (Ep 7), and Giving Day, the last morning of the old year, when each friend is given one small thing (Ep 8).
- No real-world landmarks, schools, or locations. Everything is an illustrated, invented place.

## Episodes so far

1. **Lost Rain Song**, lesson: listening. Mid cue: "Listen... what do you hear?" Live.
2. **Sleepy Moon**, lesson: patience, waiting gently. Mid cue in the approved video script: "Count with us... how many stars?"
3. **Quiet Star**, lesson: quiet courage, small lights matter. Mid cue: "Shine... soft and small." The lost creature is a firefly (Kevin, 2026-11-14).
4. **River That Whispered**, lesson: making amends, a real sorry helps fix what broke. Mid cue: "Mend... soft and true."
5. **Very Patient Tortoise**, lesson: going at a friend's pace. Mid cue: "Slow... soft and kind."
6. **Market of Small Kindnesses**, lesson: small kindnesses add up. Mid cue: "Share... soft and free."
7. **Thank-You Lantern**, lesson: a thank-you means most with a name and one true thing in it. Mid cue: "Thank... soft and warm."
8. **Gift That Could Not Be Wrapped**, lesson: the best gift can be your time. Mid cue: "Stay... soft and close."

Each episode: a new setting, a new friend or problem, a new resolution type, so no two episodes repeat the same lesson. ## Locked production decisions (Kevin, 2026-09-30 SAST, apply to Episodes 1-3 and all future episodes)

- **Voices:** three separate dedicated voices, one per language (English, Afrikaans, isiZulu). Never a single South African-accented English voice.
- **Narrator:** an old wise man with a warm, deep storytelling tone (not young, not neutral), in all three languages.
- **Art style:** looks like an old man drawing for his granddaughter. Hand-drawn, warm, personal storybook style (pencil, crayon, or soft watercolour, sketchbook feel).
- **AI disclosure:** always disclose, worded exactly "Created from Kevin's stories, brought to life with AI." No other wording.
- **Channel:** Princess Baylin Diaries (spoken "Princess Balin Diaries"), live at https://www.youtube.com/@PrincessBaylinDiaries, set as made for kids.
