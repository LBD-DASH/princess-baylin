## The call

A hundred videos in 20 weeks is workable as a catalogue of 40 distinct stories and 60 supporting videos, released five a week from the week of Monday 23 November 2026. It is not workable as a hundred look-alike uploads, which is the pattern YouTube is removing from monetisation.

## Why not a hundred fast videos

1. **Growth advice written for general channels does not fit.** A made-for-kids channel has no comments, end screens, cards, notification bell or personalised ads. YouTube lists exaggerated content and misused popular keywords as low-quality signals for kids videos, and a channel focused on low-quality kids content can be suspended from the Partner Programme ([YouTube kids quality principles](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10774223)).
2. **A hundred fast AI videos is the pattern YouTube is removing.** In January 2026 it terminated 16 channels with 35 million subscribers between them under its inauthentic content policy ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)). On 16 July 2026 it named generic, repetitive or template-based content as one of three kinds that cannot be monetised ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/)). The policy is judged across the whole channel, and what it allows is a series with recurring characters in which each video has its own storyline ([YouTube monetisation policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).
3. **Our own October drafts showed the pattern.** Episodes 4, 5 and 6 each opened with Baylin racing ahead and ended with her slowing down. The fix: the slate uses 18 story shapes, none more than four times; Episodes 5 and 6 have new openings; and Bonayo's closing line changes with the lesson.

Forty stories run as four seasons of ten, each set in a new part of the kingdom, so every five weeks the art, the cast and the thumbnails change.

| 2. The Whispering Veld | 11 to 20 | Grassland beyond the garden wall | 29 Dec 2026 to 29 Jan 2027 | New Year's Day, and a first-day-of-school story the evening before schools open on 13 Jan |
| 3. The River Road to the Sea | 21 to 30 | A boat journey with Pip to the shore | 2 Feb to 5 Mar 2027 | Valentine's week |
| 4. The Mountain of Mist | 31 to 40 | The mountain above Sunhill | 9 Mar to 9 Apr 2027 | Human Rights Day, Easter week, the autumn school holiday |

| Format | Count | Length | Job | Day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Story episode | 40 | 8 to 11 min | The IP itself, and the proof of originality | Tuesday and Friday |
| Calm Minute Short | 40 | 30 to 45 sec | Discovery, and the reel for parents | Wednesday and Saturday |
| Sunhill Song | 10 | 2 to 5 min | Replays. Three are in all three languages | Sunday, odd weeks |
| Sleepy-Time Collection | 10 | 40 min to 2 hr | Watch hours | Sunday, even weeks |

Every new friend's name in the slate is a placeholder for Kevin and the author to approve. The names are drawn from all three languages on purpose, and the Afrikaans and isiZulu names need the native-speaker check like everything else.

The calendar assumes the pipeline can finish two episodes a week. At one a week the same slate runs 40 weeks. If the launch slips, every date moves by whole weeks.

## Reels

Each episode produces one vertical video, and it is used twice.

- **On YouTube, as the Calm Minute Short**, the day after its episode. It turns the episode's calm cue into a 30 to 45 second bedtime ritual: one line of story, the cue on screen, a five-second pause for the child, then Bonayo's goodnight. It is drawn for the vertical frame, because plain story clips on comparable channels drew only a few hundred views each (faceless-youtube-content `research/2026-10-01-baylin-format-options.md`).
- **Off YouTube, re-captioned for parents**, on a Princess Baylin Diaries Facebook page and Instagram through Buffer, with one extra reel a week showing a drawing come together. A made-for-kids channel cannot notify subscribers, take comments or link from end screens, so parent-facing reels are the only growth lever the channel owns.

Shorts will not earn. From 1 February 2027 Shorts revenue needs 10 million views in 90 days, and Shorts watch time does not count toward the watch-hour bar ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/)). Cap each one at an hour of work.

2. **Canon check.** Under Kevin's delegation of 14 November a script Claude marks approved is cleared for art. Kevin and the author can still change anything before the render.
3. **Art and narration.** The existing render pipeline (Grok Bot), with the locked hand-drawn style and the old-storyteller voice.
4. **Assembly.** Captions, chapters, the disclosure card and the made-for-kids setting.
5. **Kevin watches the finished video**, about 10 minutes. This is the quality gate the README requires, and nothing is made public before it.
6. **Schedule** for late afternoon, so the episode is live before bedtime.
7. **Next day.** The Calm Minute Short and the parent reel.
8. **Monday.** The numbers go into the slate and the next fortnight is re-ranked.

Steps 3 and 4 are the unknown. The agents that ran them stopped in October, so the render pipeline has to be restarted before the calendar means anything.

## Languages

English is the master for all hundred videos. Afrikaans and isiZulu are the edge, and the only thing blocking them is a reviewer for each.

- **Why they matter.** Small channels in both languages already pull real audiences. An Afrikaans fairy-tale channel with under 7,000 subscribers had one story at 87,000 views after about a month, and an isiZulu channel has stories at 161,000 and 517,000 views, built up over several years (faceless-youtube-content `research/2026-10-01-baylin-format-options.md`). English bedtime stories are a crowded shelf. These two are not.
- **How to release them.** Since September 2025 every creator can add extra audio tracks to one video ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/youtubes-multi-language-audio-feature-for-dubbing-videos-rolls-out-to-all-creators/)). That pools the watch hours but leaves the title and thumbnail in English. A separate upload with an Afrikaans title is easier for an Afrikaans-speaking parent to find. Test both on Episode 1 and keep whichever earns more Afrikaans watch time after 28 days.
- **The rule stays.** No Afrikaans or isiZulu line goes live without a named native-speaker reviewer. Afrikaans can start the week a reviewer is named. isiZulu needs a reviewer and a narrator.

## Money

Advertising will not pay for this channel. The return is in owning a hundred-title family IP.

- **The bar.** Ad revenue needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours today, and 8,000 watch hours for anyone applying from 1 February 2027 ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/)). With one subscriber today, plan against 8,000.
- **What 8,000 hours takes.** About 120,000 views of an episode watched for four minutes, or about 24,000 views of a collection watched for twenty. Both watch times are assumptions. That gap is why a tenth of the slate is collections.
- **What it pays.** The median made-for-kids channel earned $0.33 per 1,000 views in a study of 300 monetised channels ([AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-glossary/what-does-made-for-kids-mean-on-youtube)). At that rate a million views is about $330.
- **Where the return is.** First, picture books: the Episode 1 book is already laid out for KDP in `book/`. Second, printables such as colouring pages and calm-cue cards. Third, trilingual story-and-activity packs licensed to preschools and Foundation Phase classrooms, which would be recurring revenue. That third route is a proposal and is untested. Fourth, licensing, once the catalogue and an audience exist.

## Scoreboard and stop rules

Three numbers are read every Monday, against the targets and stop rules already set in the README.

| Measure | Target | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Average view duration | 40% or more on at least one episode by day 30 | Whether the stories hold a child |
| Watch hours a week | About 155 | Pace toward 8,000 hours in a year |
| Subscribers | 100 by month 3, then 1,000 | The other half of the monetisation bar |

- **Stop** on any inauthentic-content or reused-content warning from YouTube. Pause uploads and review the format.
- **Rethink** after 12 episodes if subscribers are under 50 or average view duration is under 25%.
- **Double down** on any episode that clears 40%. It gets its song, its Afrikaans version and its picture book first.
