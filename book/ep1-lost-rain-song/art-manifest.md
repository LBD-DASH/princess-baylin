# Art manifest: Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song (Episode 1)

All pictures are existing Episode 1 art. No new art was generated for the book.

Source: the rendered English episode video on the shared box, `/workspace/princess-baylin/video/lost-rain-song-en.mp4` (1920x1080, 10:06). Its 13 illustrations were made in Kevin's Canva account in the grandfather's sketchbook style (see `/workspace/princess-baylin/video/README-production-notes.md`). The original stills (`build/img2/`) are no longer on the box, so each picture here is a full 1920x1080 frame grabbed from the video during a narration pause, so no burned-in caption is visible. Frames from the same scene differ slightly because of the slow pan and zoom in the video.

File names are `s<scene>_<seconds into the video>.jpg`. Re-extract any frame with `extract_art.sh` (needs ffmpeg and the video).
