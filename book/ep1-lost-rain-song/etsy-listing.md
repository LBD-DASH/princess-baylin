Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song
A calm bedtime picture book about listening, teamwork and saying thank you. For children aged 3 to 7.

In the Kingdom of Sunhill the garden is dry, the pond is low, and the Rainbird has forgotten the song that brings the rain. Princess Baylin thinks singing louder will fix it. With slow and steady Tilly the tortoise beside her, she visits the frogs, the grasshoppers and the Grandmother Tree, and learns that a song can be shared out in little pieces. When everyone listens, the soft rain comes home.

PRINTING TIPS
- US Letter paper: print at "Actual size" (100%). The 8.5 in page fits the width exactly; trim the extra length at the bottom.
- A4 paper: choose "Fit to page" (about 97%), then trim.
- Use thick matte paper or card (160 to 200 gsm) for a sturdier book. Photo paper works for a glossy look.
- For a double-sided book, print the odd pages first, turn the stack over, then print the even pages. Do a two-page test first.
- Bind with staples, a ring binder, or a local print shop's saddle stitch or coil binding.
- Colours look a little different on paper than on screen. That is normal.
