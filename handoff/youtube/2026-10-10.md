## Story beats (12, from approved `scripts/2026-09-30.md`)

1. **Dusk settles.** Baylin and Tilly walk the garden path. Big stars shine proud and far. One tiny light hides behind a cloud.
2. **Look again.** Baylin almost walks past. Tilly: "Look again. Soft and small." A shy glow blinks, then hides. "Hello, little light."
3. **Too small.** The Quiet Star: "I am too small. The bright stars guide ships. Who would follow a blink?" Baylin: "You are a light. Even a small one." (Light callbacks to Ep1 listening and Ep2 waiting.)
4. **Lanterns too loud.** Baylin dances and waves two garden lanterns to cheer the star. It hides completely. "I think I was being loud with light." Soft comedy, never distress.
5. **Soft glow close by.** Tilly: "Sometimes a friend needs a soft glow close by." and "Bravery can be quiet." Baylin sets the lanterns down.
6. **Lost in the grass.** A firefly friend has lost the path home through tall grass. The big stars are too high to help.
7. **One soft blink.** Baylin sits still and asks softly for one blink. A pale-gold tip of light appears.
8. **Shine soft and small (child pause).** On-screen cue "Shine... soft and small". 3 to 5 second pause. Each blink a little braver.
9. **Path of tiny lights.** Soft blinks mark the grass like stepping stones. The firefly follows home. No chase, no fear.
10. **Small can still guide.** "My light was enough." Baylin: "Small can still guide. Quiet courage counts." Tilly: "The brightest star is not always the one a friend needs."
11. **Thanks and a wink.** Baylin thanks the Quiet Star and Tilly. Optional single Sleepy Moon wink.
12. **Home and soft sleep.** Window star glow, "Thank you, friends!", goodnight to the sky, invite "What small kindness can you share today?", soft tease of a later river or sorry story, "The Quiet Star will keep a soft watch while you dream."
