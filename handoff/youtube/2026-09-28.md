## Story beats (12, calm pace, ~8–10 min narrated)

1. **Dry morning.** Baylin and Tilly find drooping flowers and a very low pond.
2. **Sad Rainbird.** On the old fig tree: "I've forgotten my rain song."
3. **Baylin tries loud.** She runs to the hilltop and sings as loudly as she can. Only a puff of dust. She feels frustrated (gentle, not scary).
4. **Tilly's advice.** "Maybe we need to listen, not shout."
5. **Frogs.** Baylin asks gently. Frogs remember the *drum* of the rain.
6. **Grasshoppers.** They remember the *patter*.
7. **Grandmother tree.** She remembers the *hum of the wind*.
8. **Almost giving up.** Baylin gets impatient. Tilly: tortoises get there in the end.
9. **Song comes together.** Frogs drum, grasshoppers patter, wind hums, Rainbird remembers the tune.
10. **Soft rain.** Clouds gather. Gentle rain. Flowers lift their heads. No storms, no fear.
11. **Puddle joy and thanks.** Baylin thanks each friend. "The song was never lost. It was shared out."
12. **Bedtime close.** Thank-you ritual. Invite: "What small thing can you do to help your friends today?" Soft fade. Optional soft tease toward Episode 2 night-sky idea (Sleepy Moon) without spoiling.
