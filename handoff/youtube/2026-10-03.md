## Lesson

Small kindnesses add up. Sharing one soft thing (a bun, a smile, a turn) can warm a whole market morning. One huge rushed kindness is not better than many small true ones.

## Story beats (12, calm pace, ~8-10 min narrated)

1. **Market wake.** Soft morning. Sunhill market opens. Baylin wants to do one huge kindness before noon. Tilly hums and follows.
2. **The big plan.** Baylin races to gather many things at once for a "grand share." Her arms are too full. Something soft tumbles.
3. **Spill.** At the first stall the big plan spills gently (fruit rolls, basket tips). No one is hurt. Baylin feels flustered, not scared.
4. **Mallow notices.** Mallow the Market Mouse offers a tiny warm bun and a calm word. "Small is enough," Mallow says.
5. **Tilly sees.** Tilly points out little kindnesses Baylin rushed past: a shared shade cloth, a turned basket, a smile for a shy shopper.
6. **Baylin slows.** Baylin puts down the huge plan. She watches. She listens to the market's soft rhythm.
7. **Mid cue.** On-screen: "Share... soft and free." Baylin offers one small kindness of her own (a turn in line, a returned fruit, a thank-you bun to Mallow).
8. **Ripple.** One small share invites another. A neighbour passes fruit. A child (illustrated friend, not a real child) offers a ribbon for a basket. The market feels warmer.
9. **Together.** Baylin, Tilly, and Mallow share a quiet stall-side snack. Bonayo may glide once above, watching kindly (optional cameo).
10. **Mallow's lesson.** "Your huge plan was kind," Mallow says. "But the market is made of small gifts that add up." Baylin nods.
11. **Thanks.** Baylin thanks Mallow, Tilly, and the soft morning market.
12. **Bedtime close.** Thank-you ritual. Invite: "Who could you share one small kindness with today?" Soft fade to Sunhill asleep under a warm market-dusk sky (lanterns soft, never scary).
