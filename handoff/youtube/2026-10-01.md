## Lesson

Making amends: a real "sorry" slows down and helps fix what broke. It is not just a word said while still running.

## Story beats (12, calm pace, ~8-10 min narrated)

1. **Race begins.** Baylin challenges Tilly to race to the willow tree and takes off running.
2. **The accident.** Baylin's feet splash through the shallow reeds, scattering a tiny home of pebbles and woven reeds she didn't see.
3. **A sad voice.** Pip the river fish surfaces, looking at his broken home. Baylin calls "Sorry!" without stopping and keeps running.
4. **Tilly catches up.** She looks at the mess and gently tells Baylin: "I think your sorry ran away before you did."
5. **Baylin realises.** She stops, looks back, sees Pip still sad. Her cheeks go warm.
6. **Baylin returns.** She walks back slowly and apologises again, this time kneeling down and asking what broke.
7. **Mid cue.** On-screen: "Mend... soft and true." Baylin places the first pebble slowly, the way Pip shows her.
8. **Working together.** Tilly hands Baylin reeds one at a time; Baylin weaves them gently instead of rushing.
9. **The home returns.** The last pebble settles; the current slips through the reeds with a soft whispering sound.
10. **Pip's lesson.** "A true sorry has hands that help, not just words that hurry past." Baylin nods, remembering it.
11. **Thanks.** Baylin thanks Tilly and Pip at the riverbank as evening comes on.
12. **Bedtime close.** Thank-you ritual. Invite: "What could you help mend today?" Soft fade to the kingdom asleep beside the whispering river.
