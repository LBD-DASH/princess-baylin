## Story beats (12, from approved `scripts/2026-09-29.md`)

1. **Wide awake.** Baylin tucked in bed; curtains open on a flat, starless navy sky. No moon. "Where is the moon?"
2. **Tilly notices.** Tilly at the door. The silver puddle of moonlight that usually sits on the floor is missing. "The night is taking its time."
3. **A moon to find.** Baylin puts on a soft cloak. "The moon must be lost... I will go and find it myself." Fetching is not yet known to be the wrong answer.
4. **Too fast on the hill.** Moonless hillside. Baylin walks too quickly, soft stumble over a root. Tilly: "We are the ones in a hurry."
5. **Bonayo speaks.** From a bare branch: "The moon rises when it is ready, not when it is called. Worry cannot pull it up. Only time can."
6. **Calling the moon.** Baylin calls upward as loudly as she can. Empty night hush. Soft comic beat (echo of Ep1 shout-first), never distress.
7. **Sit and wait.** Tilly finds a flat stone and pats the space beside her. Baylin sits. "Waiting is a skill you practise."
8. **Counting stars (child pause).** Stars blink on one by one. Mid cue: "Count with us… how many stars?" Soft pause for the child to count along (about 3 to 5 seconds).
9. **Did the moon forget?** Baylin hugs her knees. Tilly steady: "It is only sleepy. Sleepy things take longer to wake."
10. **A silver glow.** At the edge of the world, a faint silver glow begins. Baylin watches without jumping up or shouting.
11. **The moon rises.** Full moon over Sunhill. Hillside soft with silver. "Thank you for waiting with me." Tilly: "Sitting is harder than running, sometimes."
12. **Home and soft sleep.** Moonlight puddle back on the bedroom floor. "Thank you, friends!" Closing invite: "What can you wait for patiently today?" Soft Ep3 tease: Quiet Star silhouette, "small lights can be enough." Goodnight, little listener.
