# Episode 7

**Episode:** 7 (slate code S1E07, planned for Tuesday 15 December 2026)
**Episode title:** Princess Baylin and the Thank-You Lantern

## Hook line

English: Once a year, when the old year is nearly done, the Kingdom of Sunhill keeps Lantern Night. A hundred paper lanterns wait along the hill path. Each one lights only for a thank-you. Princess Baylin had the biggest thank-you in the kingdom ready. And not one lantern glowed.

Afrikaans (NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK): Een keer per jaar, wanneer die ou jaar amper verby is, hou die Koninkryk Sonheuwel Lanternnag. Honderd papierlanterns wag langs die heuwelpad. Elkeen brand net vir 'n dankie. Prinses Baylin het die grootste dankie in die koninkryk gereed gehad. En nie een lantern het gegloei nie.

isiZulu (NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK): no draft offered. This line should come from the isiZulu reviewer, working from the English.

## Lesson

A thank-you means most when it has a name and one true thing in it. Mid cue: "Thank... soft and warm".

## Characters

| Character | Role | Notes for visuals |
|---|---|---|
| Princess Baylin | Hero | Illustrated child character only. Never a real child's face. Careful from the start in this episode; she does not rush. |
| Tilly the Tortoise | Companion | Asks the question that turns the story: "Do not think of everyone. Think of one." |
| Wick the Hedgehog (new, placeholder) | The lantern keeper | Small hedgehog with a tall reed that has no flame. Line: "One name. One true thing." |
| The Rainbird, Pip, Mallow, the Quiet Star | Friends thanked | One short scene each, as drawn in Episodes 1, 4, 6 and 3. |
| Bonayo | Closes the episode | In the old fig tree. Line: "Well thanked, Princess Baylin." |

The owl is Bonayo (English and Afrikaans: Bonayo; isiZulu: uBonayo).

## Story beats

Hook. Lantern Night: a hundred unlit paper lanterns, and Baylin's big thank-you lights none of them.
1. Wick the lantern keeper straightens the lanterns with a reed that has no flame. They light themselves when they hear what they are waiting for.
2. The rule: one true thank-you, one lantern. Baylin decides to say one thank-you big enough for all hundred.
3. From the palace steps she calls, "Thank you, everyone, for everything!" The lanterns stay dark.
4. Wick: a thank-you with no name does not know which way to go. Tilly: "Think of one."
5. Baylin thanks the Rainbird for singing the rain song home. The first lantern lights.
6. She thanks Pip at the river and Mallow at the market. Each thank-you feels different to say.
7. Mid cue and child pause: she thanks the Quiet Star, and the child is asked to say one name and one true thing.
8. The thank-yous travel: friends begin thanking each other, and the hill fills with light.
9. Baylin thanks Tilly "for waiting for me. Every single time." The warmest lantern.
10. One lantern is still dark. Wick: "That one is not yours to light."
11. Her friends thank Baylin by name, and the last lantern lights. It was hers.
12. Bonayo: "Well thanked, Princess Baylin." Home, bed and the thank-you ritual.
13. Goodnight, the reflective line and the tease of Episode 8.

## Episode-specific visual notes

Blue dusk that fills with small gold lights, one lantern at a time. The key image is the wide view of the hill as the lanterns light one after another (Beat 8). The warmest light in the episode is the two-shot of Baylin and Tilly (Beat 9). Calm faces throughout; the dark lanterns are a puzzle, never a sadness.

# Episode 8

**Episode:** 8 (slate code S1E08, planned for Friday 18 December 2026)
**Episode title:** Princess Baylin and the Gift That Could Not Be Wrapped

## Hook line

English: It was Giving Day in the Kingdom of Sunhill, and Princess Baylin had a gift for everyone. Every gift was wrapped in a leaf and tied with grass. Every gift but one. Because what do you give to a tree?

Afrikaans (NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK): Dit was Geedag in die Koninkryk Sonheuwel, en Prinses Baylin het 'n geskenk vir almal gehad. Elke geskenk was in 'n blaar toegedraai en met gras vasgebind. Elke geskenk behalwe een. Want wat gee jy vir 'n boom?

isiZulu (NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK): no draft offered. This line should come from the isiZulu reviewer, working from the English.

## Lesson

The best gift can be your time: staying with someone. Mid cue: "Stay... soft and close".

## Characters

| Character | Role | Notes for visuals |
|---|---|---|
| Princess Baylin | Hero | Illustrated child character only. Never a real child's face. Does not rush and breaks nothing in this episode. |
| Tilly the Tortoise | Companion | Asks two questions: "What does the Grandmother Tree do all day?" and "Who stays with her?" |
| The Grandmother Tree (placeholder) | The friend of the episode | The old fig tree from Episode 1. Kindly face suggested in bark and leaves, never scary. Speaks "in her voice like quiet wood". |
| The Rainbird | Asks what Baylin gave | Soft grey-blue feathers, as in Episode 1. |
| Bonayo | Closes the episode | Wakes in the hollow of the fig tree. Line: "Well stayed, Princess Baylin." |

Pip and Mallow are named in passing. The owl is Bonayo (English and Afrikaans: Bonayo; isiZulu: uBonayo).

## Story beats

Hook. Giving Day: every gift is wrapped in a leaf and tied with grass, except one. What do you give to a tree?
1. On the last morning of the old year Sunhill gives one small thing to each friend. Baylin has forgotten the Grandmother Tree.
2. The tree has given all year: shade, figs, a branch for the Rainbird, a hollow for Bonayo. Baylin goes to find the best small thing in the kingdom.
3. A red ribbon. The wind takes it over the garden wall.
4. A speckled river stone. A beetle rolls it away.
5. The handsomest parcel anyone has wrapped, with nothing inside it. "I have nothing a tree can use."
6. Tilly's two questions. The tree stays, and nobody stays with her. "Will you sit a while?"
7. Mid cue and child pause: Baylin leans against the trunk and stays. The child is asked who they could sit beside.
8. She brings the river, the market and the lantern hill to the tree as stories.
9. She sweeps the old leaves from between the roots.
10. The last ripe fig lands in her lap. "Giving is what I do. But today, for once, somebody gave to me."
11. The friends arrive to swap parcels. "What did you give her?" Tilly: "The afternoon."
12. Bonayo: "Well stayed, Princess Baylin." Home, bed and the thank-you ritual.
13. Goodnight, the reflective line and the tease of Episode 9, the Knot That Grew.

## Episode-specific visual notes

Morning green under the old fig tree, turning to gold afternoon and blue evening. Baylin and Tilly are very small against the tree. The three failed gifts are gentle and funny, never losses. The empty leaf parcel stays in frame from Beat 5 to Beat 11.
