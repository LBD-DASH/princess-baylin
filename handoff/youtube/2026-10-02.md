## Lesson

Friendship moves at the pace of care. Going slowly with a friend is not losing. Racing ahead alone can mean missing the path, and the friend.

## Story beats (12, calm pace, ~8-10 min narrated)

1. **Hill call.** Late afternoon. Baylin wants to reach the hilltop lookout before the last warm light fades. She calls Tilly to hurry.
2. **The race begins.** Baylin runs ahead up the path. Tilly follows at her own pace, humming.
3. **The fork.** The path splits. Baylin picks the steep shortcut without waiting. Tilly stays on the gentle winding path.
4. **Tall grass.** Baylin's shortcut closes with soft tall grass. She is turned around, not hurt, just alone and unsure which way is up.
5. **Tilly notices.** On the slow path, Tilly sees painted pebbles and little wind markers that point the way. She keeps humming.
6. **Baylin listens.** From the grass, Baylin hears the hum. She stops thrashing and listens for her friend.
7. **Mid cue.** On-screen: "Walk... soft and slow." Baylin steps carefully toward the sound.
8. **Together again.** Baylin finds Tilly on the gentle path. She slows her steps to match Tilly's.
9. **The lookout.** They reach the hilltop together as the light turns honey-gold. The view is warm and wide.
10. **Tilly's lesson.** "I wasn't late," Tilly says softly. "I was with the path. And now I'm with you." Baylin nods.
11. **Thanks.** Baylin thanks Tilly on the lookout as evening softens.
12. **Bedtime close.** Thank-you ritual. Invite: "Who could you walk beside today?" Soft fade to the kingdom asleep under a warm evening sky.
