## Story beats (12, calm pace, ~8–10 min narrated)

1. **Almost bedtime.** Baylin is ready for sleep. The moon is still wide awake above the garden.
2. **Baylin hurries.** She tries to shush the moon with a quick goodnight. The moon only blinks brighter.
3. **Tilly's pace.** Tilly suggests sitting still together. Baylin fidgets.
4. **Too many plans.** Baylin tries counting stars too fast, then covering her eyes. Nothing helps the moon settle.
5. **Moon's wish.** Softly: the moon wants one slow story, told without rushing.
6. **Baylin starts too fast.** She races through a tiny tale. The moon yawns the wrong way (more awake). Gentle comedy.
7. **Tilly models waiting.** Tilly tells one short line… then pauses. The night feels softer.
8. **Baylin tries again, slower.** She tells a calm story about soft rain and friends (light Ep 1 callback, no re-plot).
9. **Quiet Star blinks.** One slow blink for each pause. The moon's eyelids grow heavy.
10. **Moon sleeps.** Silver eye closes. Stars dim to a night-light glow. No fear, no dark scare.
11. **Thanks.** Baylin thanks Tilly and the Quiet Star. "Waiting was the kind thing."
12. **Bedtime close.** Thank-you ritual. Invite: "What is one thing you can wait for gently today?" Soft fade. Optional soft tease toward a later river or market story without spoiling.
