## Story beats (12, calm pace, ~8–10 min narrated)

1. **Dusk settles.** Baylin and Tilly walk the garden path. The big bright stars already shine. One tiny light hides.
2. **Almost missed.** Baylin almost walks past. Tilly pauses. "Look again… soft and small."
3. **Quiet Star speaks.** Behind a soft cloud: "I am too small. The bright stars guide ships. I only blink."
4. **Baylin's first plan.** She tries to cheer the star by dancing and waving lanterns. Too much noise and light. The Quiet Star hides more.
5. **Tilly's nudge.** Soft: big lights are lovely, but sometimes a friend needs a soft glow close by.
6. **A small problem.** A firefly friend (or gentle moth; keep calm) has lost the path home through tall grass. Bright stars are too high to help.
7. **Baylin listens to quiet.** She sits still with Tilly. She asks the Quiet Star for one soft blink.
8. **Mid cue.** On-screen: "Shine… soft and small." The Quiet Star peeks out.
9. **Path of soft light.** Tiny blinks mark the grass path. The firefly (or moth) finds home. No chase, no fear.
10. **Star learns.** Quiet Star: "My light was enough." Baylin: "Small can still guide."
11. **Thanks.** Baylin thanks Tilly and the Quiet Star. Sleepy Moon may wink once (cameo only).
12. **Bedtime close.** Thank-you ritual. Invite: "What small kindness can you share today?" Soft fade. Optional soft tease toward a later river or "says sorry" story without spoiling.
