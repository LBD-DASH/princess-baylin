## Story beats (12, as rendered)

1. **Dry morning.** Baylin and Tilly find drooping flowers and a nearly empty pond.
2. **Sad Rainbird.** "I've forgotten my rain song."
3. **Too loud.** Baylin sings as loudly as she can on the hilltop. Only a puff of dust.
4. **Listen, not shout.** Tilly's advice. Mid cue: "Listen... what do you hear?" (child pause).
5. **Frogs** remember the drum. Boom. Boom. Boom.
6. **Grasshoppers** remember the patter. Tip. Tip. Tip-tip.
7. **Grandmother tree** remembers the hum of the wind. Hmmm...
8. **Almost giving up.** "Maybe I am not enough." Tilly: "Tortoises get there in the end."
9. **The song comes home.** Everyone plays their piece; the Rainbird remembers the tune; Baylin hums softly, not over anyone.
10. **Soft rain.** No storm. Flowers lift.
11. **Thanks.** "The song was never lost. It was shared out." Baylin thanks each friend.
12. **Goodnight.** "Thank you, friends!" Invite: "What small thing can you do to help your friends today?" Soft tease: "tomorrow night ... Princess Baylin meets the Sleepy Moon, and learns that waiting can be as brave as running."
