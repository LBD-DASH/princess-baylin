## Story beats (12, calm pace, ~8-10 min narrated; unchanged from approved Ep 2)

1. **Almost bedtime.** Baylin is ready for sleep. The moon is still wide awake above the garden.
2. **Baylin hurries.** She tries to shush the moon with a quick goodnight. The moon only blinks brighter.
3. **Tilly's pace.** Tilly suggests sitting still together. Baylin fidgets.
4. **Too many plans.** Baylin counts stars too fast, then covers her eyes. Nothing helps the moon settle.
5. **Moon's wish.** Softly: the moon wants one slow story, told without rushing.
6. **Baylin starts too fast.** She races through a tiny tale. The moon gets more awake. Gentle comedy.
7. **Tilly models waiting.** Tilly says one short line, then pauses. The night feels softer.
8. **Baylin tries again, slower.** A calm story about soft rain and friends (light Ep 1 callback, no re-plot).
9. **Quiet Star blinks.** One slow blink for each pause. The moon's eyelids grow heavy. Mid cue: "Wait... soft and slow."
10. **Moon sleeps.** Silver eye closes. Stars dim to a night-light glow. No fear, no dark scare.
11. **Thanks.** Baylin thanks Tilly and the Quiet Star. "Waiting was the kind thing."
12. **Bedtime close.** Bonayo's soft "Hoo-hoo", thank-you ritual. Invite: "What is one thing you can wait for gently today?" Soft fade. Optional soft nod that Ep 3 (Quiet Star) exists as an approved next watch, without spoiling.

## Production pack extras (for YouTube agent)

### Narration direction
- Pace: the slowest of the first three episodes. This episode is about waiting, so pauses are part of the story. Leave about two seconds of quiet after each Quiet Star blink and after the mid cue.
- Tone: old wise man, warm and deep. Never rushed, never jokey-young, never flat-neutral.
- Comedy beat (beat 6, Baylin racing through her tale): narrate a touch quicker for contrast, then drop back to calm. The contrast should feel funny, not frantic.
- Volume arc: soft open, gentle restlessness, quiet middle, whisper close.
- Do not invent new dialogue beyond the approved Ep 2 beats.
